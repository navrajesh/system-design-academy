This landscape is a **catalog of correctness hops**, not a 65th lesson. CAP is 2-of-3 under a named partition. Linearizability is not the default of PostgreSQL READ COMMITTED, DynamoDB eventual indexes, or Kafka.

Authoritative vs derived:

- **Authoritative:** the store or log whose uniqueness/fencing boundary the product named (SQL constraint, DynamoDB conditional write, consensus log, ledger terminal).
- **Derived:** caches, search, replica reads without a token, lock services used as caches.

Leases expire. Distributed locks are not mutexes across partitions unless the lock service is the consensus boundary and every writer checks the epoch. Cross-boundary transactions need exclusive terminals and idempotency; 2PC without a recovery story is a slogan.
