This landscape is a **catalog of latency hops**, not a 65th lesson. Profiling backends and traces are **derived telemetry**. They never become the TokenX ledger.

Authoritative vs derived:

- **Authoritative:** primary commit, object PUT acknowledgement, Kafka produce acknowledgement under the named ISR policy.
- **Derived:** Redis, CDN, read replicas, aggregation windows, dashboards, p99 traces.

Synchronous wait is the caller timeout. Queues convert load into delay; they do not create durability. Hedged requests and retries multiply work unless bounded. SLO miss budget is a named event, window, and percentile — not “we are 99.9%.”
