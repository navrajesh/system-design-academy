This landscape is a **composition map**, not a new mock interview. Module 7 prompts stay in `content/lessons/`. Here we only show which library hops those designs must name.

TokenX (fail-closed ledger):

- Authoritative: ledger rows with exclusive terminals (credited vs cancelled) and durable idempotency keys.
- Derived: Redis balances, search, analytics, Kafka copies.
- Illegal: treating a signed request log, a cache hit, or a 200 from an UNKNOWN timeout as proof of settlement.

Ad Click Aggregator (high-volume, fail-open ingest):

- Authoritative: deduped click identity (`click_id`) at the uniqueness store you name.
- Derived: windows, leaderboards, “almost real-time” dashboards.
- Illegal: stealing TokenX isolation for ads, or ads' lossy windows for money.
