<BigPicture title="The review is a cross-examination of a living system">

Prompt: you are the staff engineer reviewing TokenX plus Ad Click after a year in production. A PM wants **multi-region writes**, **10× traffic**, **a new analytics warehouse**, and **halved spend**. You have 45 minutes with an interviewer playing security, finance, and SRE.

This is not a greenfield sketch. It is [migration at scale](/learn/migration-at-scale), [cost-performance](/learn/cost-performance-engineering), [disaster scenarios](/learn/disaster-scenarios), and [driving a system design interview](/learn/driving-a-system-design-interview) applied to an existing design.

**Authoritative still:** TokenX ledger terminals; Ad Click billed log + uniqueness horizon. Everything else is a candidate to move, shard, or delete.

</BigPicture>

<UnderTheHood title="Five review moves with numbers">

### 1. Restate invariants before features

Write the invariants on the board: TokenX conservation; Ad Click click_id uniqueness horizon; p99 transfer 300 ms in the primary region. A multi-region **active-active LWW balance** is rejected before diagrams.

### 2. Demand a bottleneck with units

“10× traffic” on TokenX: 4,630 writes/s → 46,300/s. Ask whether lock wait, disk, or network is the measured limiter. If load tests still show 12,000/s headroom, 10× is a **capacity program**, not a rewrite.

<Calculation label="Cost of a fashionable Kafka add">
If analytics already consumes the outbox at 4,630 events/s × 1 KB × RF=3 ≈ 14 MB/s

A second “real-time” cluster copying the same events doubles broker and sink cost without changing conservation

Warehouse export daily 20 GB compressed plus egress: name the region and the scan job owner
</Calculation>

### 3. Failure drill

Pick one: primary region loss, poison click schema, accidental topic delete, leaked fencing token. For each: detection, fence, RPO/RTO, who pages, what is **not** auto-replayed (UNKNOWN TokenX requests).

### 4. Migration sequence

Shadow reads → dual writes with a compare job → cut reads → remove old writes. Verification samples must include TokenX conservation checksums and Ad Click invoice rebuilds. Rollback is a switch, not hope ([migration](/learn/migration-at-scale)).

### 5. Decision record

One page: context, options, chosen mechanism, invariants preserved, operational load, expiry of the decision.

<RealExamples title="Three reviews that fail without evidence">

**Multi-region TokenX.** Accept regional primary + async DR, or CRDT-free escrow. Reject LWW.

**Ad Click 10×.** Split hot campaign aggregation; keep uniqueness on click_id. Do not “just add partitions” if the sink is a single row.

**Warehouse.** Export committed outbox/log. Do not dual-write from the API into Snowflake as a second ledger.

<InterviewTraps title="Staff+ follow-ups">

- What evidence would make you shard TokenX this quarter?
- Write the rollback switch in one sentence.
- Who owns poison-message replay?
- How do you cost a derived warehouse versus a second Kafka cluster?
- What does the ADR expire?

</InterviewTraps>

<Tradeoffs title="Speed of change versus auditability">

**Ship with an ADR:** slower meeting; cheaper incident.

**Big-bang rewrite:** narrative velocity; unknown rollback.

**Optimize cost first:** may starve isolation.

**Optimize isolation first:** higher bill; safer money.

</Tradeoffs>

<InterviewAnswer>

“In a review I write invariants first: TokenX conservation, Ad Click uniqueness horizon, and the SLOs. I ask for measured bottlenecks in units before any rewrite. Multi-region writes cannot be last-write-wins on balances. Migrations dual-write with checksums and a rollback switch. I record the decision, the owner of replay, and the cost of isolation—including why ads and money do not share a bulkhead.”

</InterviewAnswer>
