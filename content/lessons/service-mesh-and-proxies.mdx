<BigPicture title="A sidecar is a policy clerk, not the bank">

[Load balancing deep dive](/learn/load-balancing-deep-dive) owns north-south VIP, Maglev, drain, and L4 versus L7. [Graceful degradation](/learn/graceful-degradation) owns bulkheads, breakers, and fail-closed money. This lesson is the **east-west proxy**: sidecar, ambient, or library, with mTLS, timeouts, retry budgets, outlier ejection, telemetry, and traffic shifting.

The data-plane proxy is a hop. The control plane (xDS) is **derived policy**. Neither is the TokenX ledger. An mTLS certificate proves a **workload identity**, not authorization to debit. Automatic retries at mesh + application + edge L7 multiply the same UNKNOWN into duplicate POSTs unless the body carries `(tenant, transfer_id)`.

</BigPicture>

<UnderTheHood title="Where the proxy sits, what it may retry, what it costs">

### Sidecar versus ambient versus library

- **Sidecar:** a proxy process per pod. Strong per-workload identity and policy. Cost is RAM/CPU per replica and a blast radius when the sidecar crash-loops.
- **Ambient / shared node proxy:** fewer processes, coarser identity unless you add a per-pod layer. Cheaper, noisier neighbor.
- **Library (Finagle, gRPC interceptors):** no extra hop, policy in the binary. Every language must implement it. You cannot patch retry defaults without a deploy.

mTLS between workloads authenticates **who is talking**. TokenX still authorizes **what they may do** in the API and still commits in the ledger. A stolen pod identity with a valid cert is a real threat; a valid cert on a debug pod must not be a debit capability. See [network security boundaries](/learn/network-security-boundaries).

### Retry amplification is multiplicative

If the edge retries twice, the mesh retries twice, and the stub retries twice, a 502 becomes up to 8× offered load. [Retries, dead letters, and replay](/learn/retries-dead-letters-and-replay) owns taxonomy. Here the rule is **one owner of retry** for a given RPC class, with a budget, jitter, and **idempotency context**.

TokenX `POST /debits` without a key: mesh retry **off**. With a key: retry only on connection errors before a response, still bounded. Ad Click ingest: retry only with the same `click_id`; fail-open to another collector is availability, not a second bill.

Outlier ejection (consecutive 5xx, latency) must keep a **min-healthy** floor. Ejecting every pod because the shared ledger 500s converts a dependency outage into an empty mesh—the same deep-health trap as the LB lesson.

<Calculation label="Sidecar cost and retry tree (illustrative)">

2,000 TokenX pods, sidecar 0.1 vCPU + 128 MB each → 200 vCPU and 256 GB RAM of proxy tax, before policy. At $0.04 / vCPU-hour (illustrative), sidecar CPU alone is ~$6k / month. That tax must buy identity and a **safe** retry default, not a hidden POST retry.

One 502 on an unkeyed debit with 2×2×2 retries: 8 POSTs. If even two lack the same `transfer_id`, you double-spend. Retry budget of **one** retry at the mesh, none at the app, none at the edge, plus a key, is 2× load in the failure case, not 8×.

</Calculation>

<MentalModel title="A court clerk who stamps passports, not contracts">

The mesh clerk checks the passport (mTLS), the appointment length (deadline), and whether to knock again (retry). The contract is signed in the vault. If the clerk knocks twice with two contract numbers, you signed twice. If the clerk’s office burns down (sidecar crash), TokenX does not let people walk into the vault unstamped.

Retain: **one retry owner, certs are identity not money, xDS is derived, fail closed when the clerk is gone on the payment door.**

</MentalModel>

<RealExamples title="TokenX fail-closed sidecar; Ad Click keyed retry">

**TokenX.** Sidecar `retryPolicy` off for POST. `timeout` less than the caller’s remaining budget. Outlier ejection with min 2 healthy. If the sidecar dies, **fail closed** (iptables reject or pod unready), not iptables ALLOW that bypasses mTLS and uniqueness. Traffic shifting 1% canary still hits the same uniqueness table.

**Ad Click.** Mesh may retry ingest **once** on connection reset with the same `click_id`. Fail-open to another collector if this one is ejected. Do not retry debug analytics produce into the billed topic. Telemetry cardinality: do not put `click_id` on a high-cardinality mesh label.

<InterviewTraps title="Mesh folklore">

- **“mTLS means the debit is authorized.”** Identity ≠ authorization ≠ commit.
- **“The mesh gives exactly-once.”** It gives retries, which are the opposite unless keyed.
- **“xDS is strongly consistent membership.”** It is a pushed photocopy.
- **“Sidecar down should fail open so we stay available.”** For TokenX that skips the clerk. Fail closed.
- **“Mesh replaces the load balancer.”** North-south Maglev math and drain remain. Mesh is east-west policy.
- **“Outlier ejection is health.”** It is a heuristic. Shared-dependency 5xx will eject everyone without a floor.

</InterviewTraps>

<Tradeoffs title="Uniform policy versus hop cost versus blast radius">

- **Sidecar versus ambient versus library:** isolation and identity versus RAM versus per-language discipline.
- **Default-deny mTLS versus allow exceptions:** safety versus break-glass and health-checker pain.
- **Rich retry versus none:** availability of reads versus duplicate writes.
- **Control plane centralization:** one place to patch policy; one place to misconfigure the fleet.

</Tradeoffs>
